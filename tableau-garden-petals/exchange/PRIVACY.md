# Garden Petals privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026.

Garden Petals is a Tableau viz extension. This is what it does with data.

- **Your data stays in your browser.** The extension reads the fields you drop on its shelves through
  the Tableau Extensions API and draws them on your screen. It does not send your data, field names or
  settings to the developer or to any third party. The code has no analytics, tracking, cookies,
  local storage or network requests of its own.
- **Settings** (colors, layout, title text) are saved inside your Tableau workbook through the
  Extensions API settings store. They travel with the workbook and nowhere else.
- **What the hosting does see.** The extension page is served from a static web host. Like any web
  host, it can log the ordinary request details (IP address, time, browser type) for the page and
  script files. It never receives workbook data.
- **Google Fonts.** The page loads the Poppins, Figtree and Bricolage Grotesque fonts from Google
  Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them. Google's privacy policy applies to that request.
- **Location lookup is local.** The garden map places flowers using a list of country and territory
  centres built into the extension. Nothing is sent to a geocoding service.

Contact for privacy questions: open an issue at https://github.com/biochristy-byte/C-Tableau/issues
