Hello Tableau Exchange team,

I would like to be added as a contributor so I can submit a viz extension, Garden Petals, to the
Tableau Exchange.

Garden Petals draws one flower per row of your data, one petal per category, with petal length set
by a measure. It has a grid view, a garden map and an altitude view, reference outlines, glow and
wilt thresholds, and a close-up bloom, all adjustable from a settings panel in the viz. It is free
and MIT licensed. The data is read in the browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
The design is inspired by *In Full Bloom* by Külli Esko, a hand-built Tableau viz.

Where it stands:
- Four encodings (Flower, Petal, Length, Score), permission "full data".
- Tested in Tableau Desktop 2026.2 and Tableau Public Desktop 2026.1 with our own hosted manifest.
- Hosted over HTTPS: https://garden-petals-viz.netlify.app/src/index.html
- Source: https://github.com/biochristy-byte/C-Tableau (folder tableau-garden-petals)
- Icon, featured images, description, privacy policy and terms are ready.
