Range Dot Settings

Quick setup
For a value that is a share of a limit, such as hours against capacity, spend against budget or sales against quota. Sets percent format, a line at 100% and four color bands. Change any of it afterward.

A small second line under a column, read from a field on Detail, such as capacity hours for the week. Most columns share a baseline, and only the exceptions need calling out.
How far from the baseline still counts as the same, as a share of it. At 1%, 39.9 against 40 is not called out.
Write text around {note}, for example {note} h cap. Also available: {baseline} and {diff}, the signed gap to it, as in {diff} h vs {baseline}.

One line across the chart at a value you set, such as 100% of capacity, a target or a budget.
In the data's own units: 1 for 100% when the data holds fractions.
Blank uses the sheet name. Text after a | is shown in regular weight.
Both lines can quote the chart, so the headline stays true when the data changes: {highlight} who the dot is, {group} the range's name, {n} columns with a dot, {above} and {below} columns where the dot is above or below the center, {over} and {under} columns above or below the line, {ref} the line's label, {category}, {value} and {member} the field names.
