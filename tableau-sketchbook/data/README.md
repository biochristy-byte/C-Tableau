# Example data

`life-expectancy-sample.csv`: life expectancy at birth, a sample.

- 54 rows: six countries (Japan, United States, India, Nigeria, Brazil, Germany) by nine
  years (1950, 1960, 1970, 1980, 1990, 2000, 2010, 2020, 2023). Values rounded to 0.1 years.
- Source: Our World in Data, "Life expectancy", https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/life-expectancy
  The series combines Riley (2005), Zijdeman et al. (2015), the Human Mortality Database (2025)
  and the UN World Population Prospects (2024).

In Tableau: Category = Year, Value = Life expectancy (years), Series = Country, Country on Detail.
